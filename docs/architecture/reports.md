# Reports

Anyone may report a message they can read, a person's profile, or a fellow member's nickname to the deployment's moderators (`app::report`, `api::report`). A report names a category from `GET /report-categories` and may explain itself in up to `EXPLANATION_MAX_CHARS` (1000) characters; the Other category needs the explanation. `POST /messages/{message}/reports` reports a message (an echo is reported as the reply it shows); `POST /users/{user}/reports` reports a profile and names the aspects found objectionable (`displayName`, `username`, `picture`, `status`, `bio`, `pronouns`), keeping the whole profile as it stood (`ProfileSnapshot`, picture id included) so a later change hides nothing; an icon a report or warning keeps cannot be deleted through `DELETE /icons/{icon}`, which takes only its uploader's icons that nothing uses (`app::icon::delete_own_icon`). `POST /communities/{community}/members/{user}/nickname/reports` reports the nickname a member chose in a community the reporter belongs to (see Roles and permissions), keeping it as it stood (`report.nickname`); a member with none is refused (`reportNoNickname`), and someone outside the community finds neither. Nobody reports their own message, profile, or nickname, or the system account's. All three answer `201` with a receipt, and share the rate limit bucket `reports` (ten back to back, fifty a day).

The categories are the rows of `report_category`: the built-in ones, named by `builtin` (`spam`, `harassment`, `hateSpeech`, `violence`, `selfHarm`, `illegalContent`, `impersonation`, `other`, seeded by the migration) and shown in the reader's language with a description, and the deployment's own, named and described in one language by holders of Manage report categories (`POST`/`PATCH /admin/report-categories`, ordered by `PUT /admin/report-category-order`). They are offered built-in first in that order, then the deployment's own by `position`, then Other. Any but Other may be hidden, which stops it being offered; categories are never deleted, so reports keep theirs.

Reports gather in cases (`report_case`), one per reported message, per reported person's profile, or per member's nickname in one community, with `kind` `message`, `profile`, or `nickname`, the `subject` whose it is, and, for a nickname, the `community`; a nickname case goes with its community, as a message's goes with its message. A case is `open` until a reviewer acts on it, `resolved` for good once one has, or `dismissed`: hidden, kept, and opened again when restored or reported again. Partial unique indexes allow one unresolved case per message, per profile, and per member and community, so a report of something with an open or dismissed case joins it (concurrent first reports make one case, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` and a locked re-read), and a report of something whose case was resolved opens a new one. A person reports one case once (`alreadyReported`, `409`). Every change to what awaits review publishes the custom `reportsChanged` event (the case, and how many of the cases the reader may see are open) to each holder of Review reports who may see the case, on their own subject.

Review takes the deployment permission Review reports. `GET /admin/reports?filter[status]=open|resolved|dismissed` pages the cases (open and dismissed by their latest report, resolved by when they were resolved; `offset` and `limit`, 15 by default) and `GET /admin/reports/{case}` reads one, each with its reports, the reported messages whether deleted or not, the categories used, and the people, channels, communities (a nickname case's among them), and attachments they name; `GET /admin/report-counts` counts the open and dismissed. `GET /admin/reports/{case}/context` reads the reported message's channel, thread, or DM around it, `CONTEXT_MESSAGES` (25) either side, deleted messages included and marked, paged with `before` and `after` no further than `CONTEXT_REACH` (100) messages from the reported one either way; each page read of a DM the reviewer is not in is written to the moderation log (`readReportContext`, naming the reported message). Nobody reviews a case about themselves, or about someone whose highest deployment role is not below theirs (`may_act`): such cases are left out of the list, the counts, and `reportsChanged`, and are not found by `GET /admin/reports/{case}` and its context, while the actions refuse them with `403` (`reportConflictOfInterest`); a case's `mayAct` is therefore true wherever it is read. Who may see a case is decided when it is read, so a reviewer who loses rank, or gains it, finds the list changed at the next read; the dashboard reads it again on each `reportsChanged`.

`POST /admin/reports/{case}/resolution` resolves an open case with one or more actions, each taking its own permission:

- `warn`: the reviewer's words, sent by the system account (`app::system_account::post`) in its DM with the subject, for the deployment's moderators and without the reviewer's name, as a message of kind `warning`, whose `warning` names the subject and the reported message, the profile as the latest report found it with every aspect the reports named, or the nickname as the latest report found it with its community and that community's name then (`NicknameSnapshot`), so it reads the same after they leave or it is deleted. Review reports allows it, so every reviewer can act on a case; the system account reaches anyone, and nobody answers or blocks it. Clients label it as a moderator's warning and show what it is about, the reported message even once deleted: message reads sideload it with `include=warnings` as `included.warnedMessages`, and only the people of the warning's DM read the warning. Logged as `warnUser`, naming the reviewer.
- `ban`: a ban from the deployment, as `PUT /admin/users/{user}/ban` takes it (see Administration). Takes Ban users.
- `deleteMessage`: deletes the reported message wherever it is (`app::message::soft_delete`), without the reviewer reaching its channel. Takes Remove content. Logged as `deleteMessage`.
- `clearNickname`: for a nickname case, clears the member's nickname in its community, whatever it is now, announced as their own clearing is (`app::community::erase_nickname`); nothing happens if they have left or cleared it. The community's ranks do not apply, the reviewer's deployment rank having been checked, so an owner's nickname can be cleared this way. It reaches foreign users too, since the nickname is this deployment's. Takes Remove content. Logged as `clearNickname`.
- `reset`: for a profile of this deployment's user, clears the named aspects (`app::user::apply_profile_update`, announced like the owner's own change); a username becomes `user-` and eight random letters and digits, and the system account tells them to choose another. Takes Remove content. Logged as `resetProfile`.

The case is marked resolved first, under its row lock, so two reviewers cannot both act; the actions follow, each recorded in the case's `resolution`, and if one fails the case is opened again, the error returned, and the actions before it stand. `PUT /admin/reports/{case}/dismissal` dismisses an open case and `DELETE` on it restores a dismissed one. Nothing deletes a report or a case through the API.

Deleting a message hides it rather than erasing it: its text, attachments, and link previews stay (`app::message::soft_delete`), so the reviews and warnings that show it still can, while every other read treats it as gone. Pruning what deletion keeps is left to the operator.
