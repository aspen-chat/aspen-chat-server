# Tagging

A message's text tags a member as `<@user-id>`, a role as `<@&role-id>`, and everyone who can see the channel as `@everyone`, wherever the message renders as text, never in code of any kind (`app::mention::parse`, which reads the message through `app::markdown`, the Markdown dialect the client renders, as the link preview scan also does). Each kind takes a channel permission: Mention members (in the member template), Mention roles, and Mention everyone (both in the Moderator and Admin templates). A tag its author may not make, or naming someone outside the community (or DM) or a role not of it, is left as plain text and counts for no one; at most `MAX_TAGS` (50) people and roles count per message. What counts is `Message.mentions` (`{users, roles, everyone}`, stored as `message.mentions` JSONB through `FromSql`/`ToSql` on `app::mention::Mentions`) and a row per tag in `mention`, written in the transaction that posts or edits the message; an edit of the text tags afresh with its author's permissions of the moment and announces the new set in the message's `update`. Message reads sideload the people tagged with `include=mentions`, as `included.users`, for a reader with no other source of names (a phone's notification code). Each `ReadState` carries `mentions`, how many unread messages tag its reader (by name, through a role they hold now, or as everyone), leaving out their own messages and those of anyone they blocked, counted through `mention`'s indexes on who is tagged; tags count in muted channels too. Which tags notify is Notifications' to decide.
