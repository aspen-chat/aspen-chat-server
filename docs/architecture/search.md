# Search

`GET /messages` searches the messages the caller may read, newest first (`app::search`, `api::message::search_messages`): the channels they may view in the communities they belong to (`Visibility::visible_channels`), the DMs they are in, and the threads of both, leaving out echoes, poll announcements, and messages by anyone they blocked. `filter[community]` or `filter[channel]` (a channel, DM, or thread, with its threads) narrows where; `filter[text]`, `filter[author]`, `filter[mentions]` (tagged by name), and `filter[has]` (`attachment`, `image`, `poll`) narrow what, and at least one of those must be given. Pages are `limit` (25, at most 50) and `before` (the last id of the previous page); `include=channels` sideloads where each was posted, threads among them. Words are PostgreSQL full-text search in the `simple` configuration, which lowercases and does nothing else, so every language is searched alike and whole words match; the text takes `websearch_to_tsquery` syntax (`"a phrase"`, `or`, `-word`). The index `message_search` is a GIN index on `to_tsvector('simple', content)` for undeleted messages, and the search writes that expression out literally rather than binding the configuration, so a prepared statement's generic plan still matches it; `message_by_author` serves the author filter. A deployment moderator searches only what they belong to, since reading another's DM is logged where it is opened. The client searches the channel or DM it is in, its community, its server, or every server the user uses, merging the last page by page (`client/packages/app/src/features/search/`).
